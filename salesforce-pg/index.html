Frequently Asked Questions

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM (customer relationship management) platform. We bring companies and customers together by providing a unified set of applications — powered by agentic AI and data — that help every department, including sales, service, marketing, commerce, and IT, work as one.

Our cloud-based agentic solutions help you find more leads, close more deals, and provide better service to your customers, all on a single, integrated platform.

CRM stands for customer relationship management. It’s a technology for managing all your company’s relationships and interactions with customers and potential customers.

  • Organize contact information, leads, and customer service data
  • Automate tasks like logging calls and sending follow-up emails
  • Analyze data to understand customer behavior and business performance

A CRM powered by AI like Salesforce gives you a single, 360-degree view of every customer.

Agentic AI refers to advanced artificial intelligence systems that can act autonomously to achieve goals with minimal human intervention.

In Salesforce, agentic AI is used to:

  • Automate complex workflows like lead qualification
  • Predict business outcomes such as deal closure likelihood
  • Personalize customer interactions across sales, service, and marketing

Salesforce offers flexible CRM solutions for businesses of all sizes — from startups to global enterprises.

You can start with essential tools like sales, service, or marketing and easily add functionality as your business grows, ensuring you only pay for what you use.
